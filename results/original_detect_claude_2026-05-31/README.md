# Original single-seed output of `detect_claude.py` (31 May 2026) — not regenerated

These four PNGs are byte-for-byte copies of `outputs_compare/fig1, fig2, fig7, fig10`
from the original thesis folder (file timestamps 31 May 2026 13:15, ten minutes after
the last modification of `detect_claude.py`). They were **not** regenerated by the
code in this repository (PyTorch was not available during preparation).

Read them with these caveats:

| Figure | What it shows | Known problems |
|---|---|---|

| `fig10_runtimes.png` | Wall-clock time: 1.1 s / 19.9 s / 20.1 s | The middle bar ("IH + PC") is PC time only (original l.1338), not IG + PC.
